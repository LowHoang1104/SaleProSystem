SaleProSystem
Problem

Managing store operations manually can make it difficult to track products, inventory, customers, suppliers, sales, and employees. This can lead to inefficient operations and difficulties in monitoring business performance.

Solution

SaleProSystem is developed to digitalize and centralize store management, allowing businesses to manage daily operations from a single system.

The system supports sales, product and inventory management, customer and supplier management, employee management, and business reporting.

Project Description

SaleProSystem is a web-based sales and store management system developed as a team project for SWP391.

The system is designed to help businesses manage their sales operations and improve the efficiency of day-to-day store management.

Main Features
Manage products and product categories.
Manage inventory and stock.
Manage sales and orders.
Manage customers.
Manage suppliers.
Manage employees and roles.
Track revenue and business performance.
Generate business reports.
Centralize store management and operations.
